Operators & Autopilot

Who runs the CMS. Operators steer through MCP and decide. Autopilot keeps things running between their visits and never publishes on its own. Magnet only talks to visitors.

Decides

Operator

Hermes, Claude, ChatGPT or you in this admin. Reads everything, writes content, approves what Autopilot proposes. Connects with an MCP key.

Maintains

Autopilot

Internal loop on a schedule: observe, plan, act, report. Keeps the blog pipeline, syncs and health checks going. Proposes; does not publish.

Talks

Magnet

Your public twin on the site. Answers from the knowledge base and hands serious leads to the inbox. Read-only.
